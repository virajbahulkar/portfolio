## DevOps lessons that compound over time

Production reliability improves when teams treat infrastructure as code, automate delivery guardrails, and make observability part of every deployment.

- Use Terraform modules to standardize cloud provisioning.
- Run Kubernetes workloads with health probes, autoscaling, and safe rollout strategies.
- Instrument systems with metrics, logs, and traces before incidents happen.
- Apply resilience patterns like retries, circuit breakers, and bulkheads.
- Practice incident response playbooks and keep security checks in the CI/CD path.
